Load only the relevant module and add project-specific questions.

## 1. Medical devices and life sciences

- Product classification, regulator, registration stage, indication, trial design, endpoints, sample size, adverse events, and data integrity.
- Ownership of clinical data, CRO/site contracts, investigator relationships, ethics, GCP, manufacturing quality, and post-market obligations.
- Separate approval from hospital access, physician adoption, reimbursement, patient affordability, and commercial revenue.
- Model addressable patients through diagnosis, eligibility, treatment willingness, capacity, price, reimbursement, and market share.
- Compare clinical evidence, workflow, total procedure cost, training, consumables, installed base, and competing therapies.

## 2. Semiconductors and hardware

- Architecture, process node, tape-out, wafer/test/packaging partners, yield, reliability, BOM, capacity, lead time, and export controls.
- Separate simulation, MPW/test chip, engineering sample, customer validation, design win, qualification, order, and volume shipment.
- Confirm IP licenses, EDA tools, open-source cores, foundry access, masks, inventory, and alternative suppliers.
- Model NRE, tape-out cycles, working capital, customer concentration, pricing, gross margin, and obsolescence.

## 3. AI, software, and enterprise services

- Data provenance, consent, security, model/IP ownership, open-source/model licenses, hallucination/evaluation, deployment, and compliance.
- Separate demo/POC from paid production deployment, acceptance, active use, renewal, expansion, and cash collection.
- Measure ARR quality, implementation labor, gross retention, net retention, churn, concentration, compute cost, gross margin, and sales efficiency.
- Test standardization versus project customization and whether customer-specific knowledge/data can be reused legally and technically.

## 4. Advanced manufacturing and robotics

- Product readiness, field uptime, cycle time, accuracy, safety, certification, BOM, yield, service burden, and failure logs.
- Separate prototype, pilot, paid trial, accepted deployment, repeat order, and scaled rollout.
- Assess component dependence, contract manufacturing, installation, warranty, spare parts, and customer workflow change.
- Model unit economics by hardware, software, integration, maintenance, and data/service revenue.

## 5. Energy, nuclear, and regulated infrastructure

- Technology readiness level, design authority, independent engineering review, safety case, testing, licensing route, siting, fuel/input, grid/offtake, and decommissioning.
- Build a regulatory critical path with decision authority, statutory sequence, precedent projects, duration, cost, and failure consequences.
- Separate conceptual design, simulation, test platform, engineering design, regulatory submission, approval, demonstration project, and commercial operation.
- Use probability-adjusted capex, financing rounds, construction schedule, cost overrun, supply chain, insurance, liability, and offtake scenarios.
- Treat policy support as context, not approval or revenue certainty.

## 6. Consumer and channel businesses

- Sell-in, sell-through, returns, rebates, inventory at channel, platform dependence, customer acquisition, repeat purchase, and cohort margin.
- Confirm channel ownership/relationships, geographic rights, pricing control, marketing subsidies, and related-party distributors.
- Model contribution margin after traffic, fulfillment, returns, discounts, and working capital.
