## 1. Report types

- `comprehensive_ic` / `report_type=comprehensive`: integrated business, financial, legal, technical, valuation, risk, and recommendation report; all P0 and comprehensive fields must pass the completeness gate.
- `business`: market, product, customers, business model, competition, operations, and commercial validation.
- `financial`: accounting quality, revenue, margins, working capital, cash flow, debt, tax, related parties, forecast, and funding needs.
- `legal`: entity, ownership, governance, contracts, compliance, employment, IP, litigation, permits, data, and transaction implications.
- `technical`: architecture, performance, maturity, reproducibility, manufacturability, IP, roadmap, dependencies, and failure modes.
- `screening_public` / `report_type=screening`: short preliminary view based mainly on public evidence; never use `deta_v5_up_to_ic` or claim full diligence.
- `pre_ic`: decision-focused synthesis only after its P0 fields pass the completeness gate.

## 2. Comprehensive structure

Use this order unless the user or project requires a justified variation:

1. 封面
2. 目录
3. 名词释义
4. 投资概要与建议
5. 公司、股权及治理
6. 核心团队与组织能力
7. 产品、技术及知识产权
8. 业务模式、客户验证与供应链
9. 行业、市场空间与竞争格局
10. 经营质量、财务分析与资金需求
11. 发展规划与预测模型
12. 估值、投资回报与退出
13. 法律合规及其他专项发现
14. 风险矩阵与交易保护
15. 尽调结论、交割条件与投后监控
16. 尽调缺口、来源及附件索引

Omit a chapter only when out of scope and say so. Never leave required headings blank.

## 3. Decision page

Allow a reader to decide within ten minutes. Include:

- project, legal entity, round, amount, pre/post-money valuation, structure, expected ownership;
- decision and confidence level;
- thesis table: conclusion, evidence, investment implication, remaining uncertainty;
- risk table: fact, probability, impact, trigger, control, residual risk;
- return scenarios and critical assumptions;
- conditions precedent, post-closing covenants, and walk-away issues;
- unresolved gaps that could reverse the decision.

## 4. Chapter questions

### Company and governance

- Is the investment entity the entity that owns people, IP, contracts, revenue, cash, and licenses?
- Who controls votes, boards, reserved matters, options, and related entities?
- Do historical financings, redemptions, side letters, liens, or unpaid capital create exposure?

### Team

- Are founders full-time, legally able to serve, and bound by IP/confidentiality/non-compete obligations?
- Which capabilities depend on one individual or external institution?
- Is hiring plan consistent with funding and milestones?

### Product and technology

- What customer problem is solved, at what measurable performance and total cost?
- What is independently tested, reproducible, manufacturable, and deployable?
- Which components, data, open-source licenses, patents, suppliers, or approvals are critical dependencies?

### Customers and business model

- Who pays, who uses, who approves budget, and who signs acceptance?
- Separate pipeline stages and reconcile contracts to delivery, acceptance, revenue, invoices, and cash.
- What are concentration, repeat purchase, sales cycle, implementation burden, gross margin, and churn?

### Market and competition

- Calculate TAM, SAM, and SOM with transparent drivers, not a single headline number.
- Compare substitutes and internal development, not only named start-ups.
- Explain why the company captures value despite incumbent response, price pressure, and customer bargaining power.

### Finance and forecast

- Reconcile statements, ledgers, tax, bank, contracts, and operational data.
- Explain margins, working capital, cash burn, runway, debt, contingent liabilities, and funding need.
- Build forecasts from operational drivers and milestones; include downside/base/upside cases.

### Valuation and return

- Normalize comparables for business model, stage, geography, growth, margin, liquidity, and cycle.
- Model dilution, exit timing, multiple compression, preference stack, taxes/costs when material, MOIC, and IRR.

### Risk and terms

- Identify thesis-breaking risks, not generic lists.
- Convert controllable risks into conditions, covenants, milestones, information rights, indemnities, price adjustments, staged funding, or walk-away rights.

## 5. Appendices

Include only decision-useful detail: cap table, financing history, customer pipeline, interview log, financial statements, forecast assumptions, valuation comps, IP schedule, material contracts, legal issue list, source list, and evidence/gap index.
